# ✍️ Volunteer brief: handwriting the CS201 answer sheets

Thank you for helping! 🙏 GradeWise is an AI that reads **handwritten** exam answers and marks them. To teach (and test) it honestly, we need real handwriting, not fonts. You'll pretend to be a student at the fictional **Northbridge Institute** sitting the **CS201 Systems Fundamentals midterm**, and write one answer script by hand. It takes about 45–60 minutes.

**You will need:** the question paper (`data/exams/cs_midterm/question_paper.pdf`), the model answers (`model_answers.md`) for reference, the profile you've been given below, A4 paper (ruled or plain), and a blue or black pen. Please also sign the [consent form](CONSENT_FORM.md) before you start.

## 1. Your student profile

We need 10 scripts across 4–6 volunteers, so some of you will write two. Please write *in character*: the mix of good and weak answers is what makes the test useful.

| Script | Profile | What to write |
|---|---|---|
| `student_01` | 💪 Strong | Answer everything, close to the model answers but **in your own words**. Aim for ~45/50. |
| `student_02` | 💪 Strong | Answer everything well; get one small thing wrong (e.g. forget to subtract 2 in Q7(a)). ~42/50. |
| `student_03` | 💪 Strong | Strong, but answer **out of order** (e.g. start with Q8, then Q1…). Short, efficient answers. ~40/50. |
| `student_04` | 🙂 Average | Main ideas right, missing details; one numerical slip carried through (e.g. use 12 offset bits in Q5). ~32/50. |

## 2. How to write

- 🏷️ **Label every answer** with its question number and part, exactly like this: **Q4(b)**. Put the label at the start of the answer, on its own line or before the first sentence.
- 🔀 You **may answer in any order**. If an answer continues later, write "Q8 (continued)".
- 🖊️ Use **blue or black pen** on **A4**. Write on one side of the page only.
- ✏️ To remove something, **cross it out with a single line** (don't scribble it into a black block, and don't use correction fluid).
- 🔢 For calculations (Q5, Q7), **show your working**, unless your profile says not to.
- 🧑‍🎓 At the top of the first page write **only** the script ID, e.g. `student_04`. **Never write your real name** (or anyone else's) anywhere on the script.
- 🚫 Do not copy the model answers word for word. Real students paraphrase, and the AI must cope with that.
- 📄 Aim for **2–4 pages** per script.

## 3. How to scan or photograph

We *want* varied quality, because real teachers scan in many ways. Pick the method you've been given:

| Scripts | Method |
|---|---|
| 01, 04, 07 | Flatbed scanner, 200–300 DPI, black and white or colour |
| 02, 05, 08 | Phone scanning app (Adobe Scan, Microsoft Lens, Notes on iPhone), good light |
| 03, 06, 09 | Plain phone photos, slightly angled, normal room light |
| 10 | Phone photo in poor light, a little out of focus (but still readable) |

- Combine all the pages of one script, **in page order**, into **one PDF**.
- Name it exactly `student_01.pdf` … `student_10.pdf` (lower case, two digits).
- Check that the photo shows no faces, names, ID cards or anything else personal in the background.
- Send the PDFs to Akshat (shared folder link in your invitation email). Please **keep the paper originals** until the course is published, in case we need a re-scan.

## 4. Privacy, in one line

No real names, no real student data, and you can withdraw your handwriting at any time **before publication** (see the consent form). Thank you, you're helping thousands of learners build something real! 🎉
